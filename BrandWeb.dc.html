About

Hi! I'm Ofri :)

Brand & motion designer with a multidisciplinary background in visual communication. A Shenkar graduate with academic honors.

I also have experience in product and UX/UI design, including enterprise SaaS products at Earnix, and a background in cybersecurity at ZIM.

I combine systems thinking with attention to detail to create cohesive brands and experiences, from core identity to micro-interactions.
